# Character art

baseball-portraits.png is an original AI-generated transparent, vector-style portrait atlas. It is raster artwork, not an editable SVG. Quadrants: Rays player, Cubs helmet batter, umpire, ball boy. Club insignia identify the depicted baseball outfits; characters are fictional. Rival appearances cycle by stadium and do not change AI difficulty. Player portrait uses the Rays cap.

The ray is real 3D geometry, not this atlas: src/ray_model.hpp builds a continuous closed upper/lower body with swept fins, smooth normals, colored dorsal surface and ivory underside. art.hpp adds sculpted facial features, a cap, a tapered animated tail and the existing tube. Ready stance turns the whole animal toward the camera; rolling faces down-course with banking. Its physics collision body, sling controls, lane dimensions and speed remain unchanged.
